Common questions

Paver Cleaning FAQ

Will cleaning blow the sand out of my paver joints?

Not the way we do it. Aggressive wand washing is what excavates joints. We use the correct pressure and technique to clean the paver surface while leaving the joint sand in place.

Why do my pavers look faded?

Usually it isn't fading at all — it's a film of algae, mildew, and grime hiding the pigment. Cleaning removes that layer and the original color comes back with it.

How often should pavers be cleaned in Florida?

Once or twice a year for most Treasure Coast homes. Shaded areas and pool surrounds stay damp longer, grow algae faster, and can get slippery, so they may need attention more often.
