Trash Bin Cleaning FAQ

Why do trash bins need professional cleaning?

In Florida heat, bins become a breeding ground for bacteria, maggots, and odor within weeks. Rinsing with a hose spreads the mess around; pressure washing plus sanitizing actually removes the source and the smell.

How much does bin cleaning cost?

$10 per bin on its own, or just $5 per bin when added to any other service — most customers tack it onto a house wash or driveway cleaning.

How often should bins be cleaned?

Most customers add it to their regular exterior cleaning once or twice a year, which keeps the bacteria and odor from ever getting established.
