Common questions

Deck & Fence Cleaning FAQ

Can you pressure wash a wood deck without damaging it?

Yes — with the right pressure and technique. Too much pressure furs and splinters wood grain, which is why we match the method to the material and soft wash where the surface calls for it.

Do you clean vinyl fences?

Yes. Vinyl fences collect algae and mildew year-round in Florida's humidity, and they come back to bright white with the correct wash. Wood and aluminum fences too — each with the method that suits the material.

How is deck and fence cleaning priced?

Decks are priced by square footage at $0.20 per square foot starting at $150. Fences are priced by linear foot at $1.00 per foot starting at $100. We text you a firm price before we schedule anything.
